Research

I develop analytical methods for the relativistic two-body problem, with applications to compact binaries and gravitational-wave modelling.

Finite-time evolution and the Magnusian

I work on describing classical dynamics through finite-time evolution maps, including the effects of radiation reaction. This provides an alternative perspective on the long-term evolution of compact-binary systems.

Gravitational self-force

I study perturbative methods for describing the motion and gravitational interactions of small bodies in curved spacetime, including conservative and dissipative effects.

Nonlocal dynamics and regularization

Another part of my research concerns nonlocal action principles and techniques for isolating physically meaningful quantities in perturbative gravity.
