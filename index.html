About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute) in Potsdam, Germany.

My research focuses on analytical approaches to the relativistic two-body problem, including gravitational self-force theory, radiation reaction, and the dynamics of compact binaries.

Biography

I received my PhD in Physics from Cornell University and am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Albert Einstein Institute.

Research

My research concerns the analytical description of relativistic compact-binary dynamics and gravitational-wave generation.
