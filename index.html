About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute) in Potsdam, Germany. My research focuses on the dynamics of compact binary systems and gravitational-wave theory.

Research

My interests include gravitational self-force, analytical methods for relativistic two-body dynamics, and finite-time descriptions of classical evolution.
