Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute) in Potsdam, Germany.

I received my PhD in Physics from Cornell University in 2025. Before that, I studied physics at the Universidad de Buenos Aires, Argentina.

My research interests lie in general relativity and gravitational-wave physics, particularly analytical descriptions of compact-binary dynamics.
